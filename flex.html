FLEX ACADEMY

A responsive landing page connecting new and existing rental operators with relevant programmes and signup paths.

Project Overview

Flex Academy introduces short-term rental education backed by the operating experience of The Flex and Base360. The brief called for a credible new brand and distinct journeys for first-time operators and people already managing rentals.

I independently designed the information architecture, responsive UI, and interactive prototype. The page prioritises webinar registration for newcomers, with separate strategy-call and checklist paths for visitors with different needs.

01 / Problem & Audience

The Challenge

The page needed to establish credibility with visitors sceptical of online courses and help two audiences identify the right programme. The design had to distinguish their needs while keeping Launch, the brief’s priority, prominent.

Launch / First-time Operators

Guidance for preparing the first property.

Visitors starting alongside a full-time job, with no rentals or one unit, need guidance on preparing for their first property.

Primary action: Register for the free webinar.

Scale / Existing Operators

Operating support for the next stage.

Visitors already managing rentals need operating systems and support to reduce manual work and prepare for growth.

Primary action: Book a free strategy call.

02 / Design Decisions

Design Decisions

Hero

Prioritising Webinar Registration

I made webinar registration the primary CTA to reflect the brief’s focus on first-time operators. The headline identifies the business opportunity, while supporting copy addresses starting alongside a full-time job. A secondary strategy-call CTA gives existing operators a relevant alternative.

Programme Comparison

Differentiating Launch and Scale

I used an approximately 3:2 card-width ratio to prioritise Launch, with equal card heights and aligned buttons to support comparison. Audience-specific headings help visitors self-select; programme descriptions distinguish first-property preparation from support for existing operations.

Credibility

Connecting Claims to Operating Experience

I paired The Flex and Base360 logos with operating facts and source links, explaining the experience behind the Academy. These claims remain attributed to the original businesses, rather than being presented as Academy student outcomes.

FAQs

Addressing Questions Before Signup

Four visible answers address programme fit, starting alongside a job, existing rental experience, and the relationship between the brands. Placing them before registration lets visitors review these questions without opening additional panels or leaving the page.

Registration

Using One Consistent Destination

All webinar CTAs lead to the same inline form. This keeps registration connected to the programme information and avoids competing signup destinations. The form requests essential details, while a secondary strategy-call option remains available.
